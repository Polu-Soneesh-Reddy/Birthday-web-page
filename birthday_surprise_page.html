💌 From the Heart

A Letter For You

Dear Nimisha,


I don't even know how to start this, so I'll just be honest — which, knowing me, you probably didn't expect.


We met in class. Nothing dramatic, nothing cinematic. Just two people in the same room, probably staring at a board neither of us fully understood. And somehow, out of all the people in that room — it was you.


My first impression of you? Honestly... I didn't know what to make of you. But I kept coming back to annoy you anyway. Repeatedly. Professionally, even. And the fact that you didn't block me or walk away — that told me everything I needed to know about the kind of person you are.


There's one thing I'll never forget though — and no, it's not the holi incident where we successfully made you disappear like a ghost 😂. It's the quiet moment when you just... massaged my head when I needed it. No drama, no big speech. Just that. And somehow that small thing said more than most people manage in years.


You have this way of caring about people that you don't even advertise. No announcements. No conditions. You just show up and make things okay — even when you yourself are simultaneously falling down somewhere, having a health crisis, and making it funny without even trying. Nimisha, you are a walking comedy and a warm hug at the same time and I genuinely don't know how you do it.


I want you to know something I should've said more clearly before — you are never left out. Not from the plans, not from the group, not from any of it. We are always by your side, even when it doesn't feel that way. Always.


For this year and everything beyond — I just want you to have more of the good stuff. Less of the falling down, more of the standing tall. Less of the health scares, more of the moments that make you laugh until you can't breathe. You deserve a year that matches the energy you give to everyone around you.


And Nimisha — everything about you is funny, chaotic, genuine, and irreplaceable. Don't change a single weird thing.


— Soneesh ❤️
